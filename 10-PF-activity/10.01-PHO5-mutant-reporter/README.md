We have three _PHO5pr_-mCherry reporter integrated in the genome. In addition to the wild type (WT) version, we also have two mutant version: in M1, the exposed weak affinity motif (UASp1) was ablated, while in M2, both the exposed weak affinity motif and the nucleosome-occluded consensus motif (UASp2) were mutationally ablated. The goal is to determine:

1. Can either ScPho4 and CgPho4 activate the reporter expression without UASp1?
2. If either or both can, do they need UASp2?
